What Is It

The 17-Character Vehicle ID

Your Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) is a unique 17-character code that identifies your Honda — the year it was made, the model, the engine, where it was built, and its exact production sequence.

  • WMI (1-3): Manufacturer and country of origin
  • VDS (4-8): Body style, model line, and engine
  • Check digit (9): Validates the entire VIN
  • Model year (10) and assembly plant (11)
  • VIS (12-17): Sequential production number
  • 100% accurate decoding from factory records
