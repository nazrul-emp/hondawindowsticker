Guide

How to Read a Honda VIN Number

A standard Honda VIN has 17 characters. Each section tells you something different about the vehicle's factory identity. VINs do not use the letters I, O, or Q.

1-3

World Manufacturer Identifier (WMI)

First 3 characters — country of origin and manufacturer.

4-8

Vehicle Descriptor Section (VDS)

Characters 4-8 — make, engine, model, restraint, trim, transmission.

9-17

Vehicle Identifier Section (VIS)

Characters 9-17 — model year, plant code, and serial number.

Characters 1–3: World Manufacturer Identifier

The first character often points to the country or region: 1,4,5 United States · 2 Canada · 3 Mexico · J Japan. The second character is commonly H for Honda. The third describes vehicle type.

Characters 4–8: Vehicle Descriptor Section

Characters 4 through 8 describe the vehicle. Depending on the Honda model and year, this part of the VIN may include information about:

  • Model
  • Body style
  • Engine type
  • Restraint system
  • Series or trim
  • Transmission type

This is where a Honda VIN search is useful. Instead of relying only on a seller's listing, you can decode the VIN and compare the result with the vehicle in front of you.

Character 9: Check Digit

The ninth character is the check digit. It helps verify that the VIN follows the correct structure.

This character does not describe the model, trim, engine, or plant. It is mainly used to help detect invalid VINs or typing mistakes.

Character 10: Model Year

The tenth character identifies the model year. This helps confirm whether the vehicle year matches the listing, title, or registration.

Character 11: Assembly Plant

The eleventh character identifies the assembly plant. Honda has used different plant codes across different years, models, and vehicle types.

Plant codes can vary, so the most reliable approach is to run the full VIN through the decoder.

Characters 12–17: Serial Number

The final six characters are the unique production sequence. This number separates one Honda from another within a production run.

Together, these characters help identify the specific vehicle, not just the model or trim.
