What Is It

What is a Honda Build Sheet?

Build sheet of Honda displays the factory configuration associated with the particular VIN. It can be used to determine the initial configuration of the car in terms of trim, package, accessories, paint color, interior trim, engine, transmission, wheels, tires, and other build options available.

It will be useful for used Honda buyers, sellers, mechanics, and enthusiasts, as it will allow checking if the car remains in its original configuration from the factory.

Difference Between Honda Build Sheet and Honda Window Sticker

Many people confuse a Honda build sheet with a Honda window sticker. They are related but not the same thing.

Honda Build Sheet

A detailed internal factory document. It focuses entirely on technical parts, part numbers, and engineering codes presented in raw format.

Honda Window Sticker

Also called a Monroney label — the sticker placed on a new car's window. It shows MSRP, standard equipment, fuel economy, and safety ratings in an easy-to-read layout.
