What Is It

What Is a Honda Warranty Coverage?

Honda warranty coverage protects vehicle owners against manufacturing defects in materials or assembly. It covers specific parts and systems for a set period of time or number of miles, whichever comes first. Every new Honda comes with a factory warranty, and many used Hondas still have active coverage too.

New Vehicle Limited Warranty

Bumper-to-bumper protection for 3 years or 36,000 miles. Covers original parts and factory electronics against unexpected mechanical failures.

Powertrain Warranty

Protects the engine, transmission, and drivetrain. Lasts 5 years or 60,000 miles, whichever comes first.

Emission Warranty

Covers emission-related parts under federal and California rules. Up to 8 years or 80,000 miles depending on the part and your state.

HondaTrue Certified Pre-Owned

Certified used Hondas include extended powertrain coverage up to 7 years or 100,000 miles from the original sell date.

Corrosion Warranty

Rust Perforation Warranty protects sheet metal parts for five years, with no limit on mileage.

Roadside Assistance

Some Hondas also include roadside assistance for a specific time and mileage period. Shown on your Window Sticker when new.
