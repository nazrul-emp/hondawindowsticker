What Is It

The Monroney Label Explained

The window sticker — officially called a Monroney label — is a federally required document affixed to every new vehicle sold in the United States. It's your vehicle's birth certificate, showing exactly what it contained when it left the factory.

  • Manufacturer's Suggested Retail Price (MSRP)
  • Factory-installed options and packages
  • EPA fuel economy estimates (city/highway/combined)
  • Standard equipment and safety features
  • Destination and delivery charges
  • Warranty information
