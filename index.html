Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Honda window stickers and VIN decoding.

A window sticker, officially called a Monroney label, is a required label affixed to all new vehicles sold in the United States. It displays the manufacturer's suggested retail price (MSRP), standard equipment, factory-installed options, fuel economy ratings, and safety information.

Your Honda VIN can be found on the dashboard near the windshield (driver's side), on the driver's side door jamb sticker, on your vehicle registration, on your insurance card, and on your vehicle title.

Most window stickers are generated and available for download within 30 seconds. In rare cases, it may take up to 2 minutes for older or less common models.

Yes. We source all window sticker data directly from official Honda factory records. Every sticker is a true replica of the original Monroney label that came with your vehicle.

We support all Honda models sold in the United States from 1980 to the current model year. This includes Accord, Civic, CR-V, Pilot, Odyssey, Ridgeline, Passport, HR-V, Fit, Insight, and more.
